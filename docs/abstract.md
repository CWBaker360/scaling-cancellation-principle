# Abstract

This note separates two geometric error-suppression mechanisms. The first is
scaling-cancellation: Richardson-type linear combinations of fixed-resolution
approximants cancel leading terms in an even-power expansion and increase the
order of accuracy. The second is nonlinear residual-map iteration in the Baker
\(4{:}3\) trisection cascade, where the signed error obeys a cubic local law.

The contribution is taxonomic rather than a claim of new Richardson theory:
fixed-resolution scaling-cancellation raises asymptotic order, while the
trisection cascade multiplies local error order under iteration.
